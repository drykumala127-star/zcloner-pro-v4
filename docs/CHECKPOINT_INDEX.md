## Known Limitations (Truthfully Recorded)
- Unrooted Android does not support third-party `/data/data` extraction, runtime DEX hooking, hardware ID spoofing (IMEI/Serial/MAC), or Play Integrity/DRM bypass; those are marked `LIMITED` or `UNSUPPORTED`.
- Split APK bundles (`hasSplitApks`), `sharedUserId` apps, and APK without DEX entries are hard-blocked (`UNSUPPORTED`) for single-APK cloning (`FMSCompatibilityEngine` & `FMSRepackager`).
- Cloud Functions for server-side billing/tier upgrades are not provisioned in AI Studio; `firestore.rules` enforces `FREE` tier lock (`deviceLimit = 3`) against client self-escalation.
- **Physical Device / Emulator Audio & UI Playback**: Because no physical Android hardware or `adb` emulator is accessible to the build agent, on-device MP3 speaker output and real device installation/launch handoff of cloned APKs with `DhedrySplashActivity` have not been hardware-verified yet.
